The 911 Legacy

Conceived by Ferdinand “Butzi” Porsche in 1963 as the Type 901, the 911 challenged convention by mounting an air-cooled flat-six behind the rear axle. Six decades later, that provocative architecture remains the most celebrated sports car footprint on Earth.

ERA CODE TYPE 901 / ORIGINAL
1964 – 1973 Foundational Icon

1964 Original 911

The birth of an immutable silhouette.

Presented as the successor to the 356, Ferdinand “Butzi” Porsche’s masterpiece debuted with a 2.0-liter, air-cooled boxer-six engine delivering 130 hp. With its fastback flyline, steep windscreen, and upright circular headlamps, it laid down the aesthetic DNA that would guide automotive design for the next six decades.

Engine 2.0L Air-Cooled Flat-6
Power 130 HP
0–60 MPH 8.3 sec
Top Speed 131 MPH
